Findings that need more than the existing design:

- Owner-level application power was recorded, but its effect on customer-controlled approval and conformance labels was not settled. Preserve that requested power; clarify the meaning of its records, not whether the user may have it.
- Signed paper is accepted, but authority/hold checks during outages and the timing of later entry were not defined. Paper evidence and permission to make a consequential decision while disconnected are different questions.
- A/B are separate welds on one assembly, not two physical coupons. Source import grouping, required coupon counts, shared specimens and combined purposes still need explicit rules.
- Initial missing qualifications appear in readiness before the trigger-routing stage; the route to C without implying permission for production needs careful treatment. Re-entering H during its own A/B/C verification also needs a defined response rather than recursive duplicate holds.
- B's validity/retest flow does not explicitly settle early NDE rejection, a partial failed test set, a later report correction or whether an invalid replacement test can use the pending-results route.
- Accepted NCR disposition, normal conformity, conditional shipment and owner exception need distinguishable outcomes. The chart's “conforming or approved” release wording must not be reduced to a single generic PASS flag.
- Correcting/reopening records after approval, release or shipment requires dependency/impact review, not just creating a new version and forgetting its downstream uses.
- The delivered workbook has no active cell formulas or external workbook links. Source `=TODAY()` appears as text in provenance notes, not a currently executing formula. Coupon Register validation stops at row 111, before all 20 M1 rows (112–131); Lists is hidden. Spreadsheet validation is not proof of controlled or accepted data.
- Source 9-1 and M1 Pass Data are referenced but absent. The original historical evidence cannot be reconstructed or independently verified from this delivery alone.
