Read with [gap register](gap-analysis.md), [schema](relational-schema.md), [authority matrix](authority-and-qualification.md) and [decision log](decision-log.md). This review distinguishes defined source responsibility from an assigned application custodian; no missing signer/custodian is invented. “Owner” below means responsible record organization/role, not unrestricted software owner.

## Unnecessary entry to avoid

Reuse person/machine/lot/WPS/drawing identities, pass actuals and test reports across registers/PQRs/travelers/dossiers through links. Do not copy an accepted result onto different hardware or convert a planned setting into a measured actual. Use stage-specific entry so coupons can exist before lab reports and approvals exist. No mandatory individual routine-A passing log; no compulsory per-weld continuity log without an approved requirement. Capture actual activity from an agreed source rather than adding data entry by default. Printed/signed records and assisted entry are first-class sources with attribution, not a second editable master.

A07 is resolved as no compulsory platform constraints currently known, with later customer checks before operational deployment. A08 impact flags and pause of further affected approvals/releases/shipments are confirmed D22; historical records remain unchanged and process/hardware authorities retain their established responsibilities. Detailed impact assessment/clearance remains B33–B35.
