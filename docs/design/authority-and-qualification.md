This role belongs to the user personally, not automatically to every developer or AM Kreations member. Organizations remain responsible for correcting their own mistakes.

Ordinary organizational and administrative roles remain scoped. The user personally has an unrestricted owner role by explicit latest direction, including approval/override of application decisions across organizations. This is an explicit exception to the original software privilege restriction; it is not extended to developers generally. Day-to-day approvals and corrections remain with the organizations.

Represent owner intervention separately from the normal Rev C decision path: preserve the original result/restriction/decision and attribute the override to the owner with target, time, reason and effect. Do not impersonate a Cone/Picogrid signer or rewrite a failed measurement as a passing measurement. These traceability mechanics preserve the requested auditable records; they do not require another organization's software approval before the owner can act. The normal routing/authority baseline and source documents retain their original status; the requested owner exception does not silently revise their text or establish production approval of those documents.

## Proposed authorization model

1. Authenticate a named person; memberships associate them with organizations. Shared sign-in accounts are not proposed.
2. An `AuthorityGrant` identifies action, program, organization, subject/scope, effective interval and evidence of designation. Senior organizational approvers are the intended internal authority structure; senior approvers manage their own organization's backup grants/revocation and self-approval restrictions within procedural scope (D20); actual designation evidence/individual scopes remain B19/B46. The personal owner role bypasses ordinary application action/organization restrictions through a separately attributable override action (Q19 resolved as product direction).
3. Check the actor's effective grant and the exact target revision at decision time on the server. A broad read grant is separate from edit, approval, disposition and release grants for ordinary users; recognize the explicit personal owner role separately.
4. Record decision type, target revision, actor, authority used, decision/conditions, time and evidence. Distinguish authenticated approval from entry of an externally signed approval. Signed paper is accepted (D09); identity/authority/timing details remain B36 and controlled outage procedure B45.
5. Revocation prevents future action without erasing valid historical attribution. Effects on existing qualifications/approvals require a controlled decision rather than automatic retrospective invalidation.
6. Self-approval is allowed initially, as explicitly directed by the user. This permission only allows an otherwise authorized approver to approve their own work: the person must hold the effective authority for that decision type and scope, and all applicable qualification, test, evidence and acceptance requirements still apply. Self-approval grants no additional authority and is not an owner override. Provide a versioned policy allowing the responsible authority to require a different approver later, scoped by organization and decision type. Apply consistently to paper and digital approvals; compare the actual performer/preparer with the approver, not merely the assisted-entry user. Record the policy revision used. New restrictions apply to subsequent approval decisions without retrospectively rewriting earlier approvals. Each organization's senior approver may restrict this policy within its own authority (D20); the personal owner retains the requested unrestricted authority.

Audit access supports authorized investigation; it does not justify logging routine passing strike plates contrary to P A.3. Broad visibility does not expose passwords, tokens or application secrets.

## Qualification matrix

| Qualification type | Subject / identity | Trigger | Required evidence and approver | Scope/continuity representation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Procedure WPS/PQR | Identified WPS revision supported by separate PQR evidence | New/revised WPS or essential-variable change per C; development D where needed | Always B through C, complete valid accepted results, Picogrid approval | Joint/material/process/parameter limits tied to controlled evidence; no inferred range from one successful trial |
| Operator WPQ | Named person, not merely a user account | New; inactivity >6 months; lost qualification; training need from H | C→B; Cone designated approval | Separate award/scope/limits and activity references; exact inactivity boundary/calendar calculation and evidence source TBD |
| Equipment | Unique physical machine ID/serial, not model | Every new/replacement machine even identical; applicable major maintenance | C→B; Cone designated approval | Award linked to unique machine and relevant setup/scope; no generic periodic expiry invented |
| Combined case | Multiple subjects/types in a planned qualification case | Several triggers apply | Shared evidence only with full approved coverage; separate authority for each award | One case may issue several awards; procedure approval does not automatically qualify operator/machine |

Proposed award states: `draft`, `awaiting_evidence`, `awaiting_approval`, `qualified`, `not_qualified`, `qualification_lost`, `requalification_required`, `superseded`. These are software labels for evidence/authority, not invented QC categories. Preserve evidence validity separately. Do not use workbook “Expired” as a universal time-based rule; only an approved scope/continuity policy can cause a time-based restriction.

Operator and equipment qualification records are not the same as authority to inspect, accept a coupon or release hardware. Formal inspection credentials are not specified in the supplied sources and must not be inferred from welding qualification.

## Manual and assisted records

User clarification supports manual work and someone helping with digital entry. Proposed model distinguishes `performed_by`, `performed_at` (including unknown/approximate date when appropriate), `entered_by`, `entered_at`, `reviewed_by` and `approved_by`. A paper scan is source evidence, not automatic digital approval. A helper cannot impersonate the worker or sign on the worker's behalf through a shared session. The user accepts signed paper for assisted entry and review/approval by a person with the appropriate action/scope authority. Attach the signed source and identify the worker, entry person and approver; no additional worker digital confirmation is required solely because the record was entered by a helper. A signed source does not grant its signer or entry person new approval authority. Do not make digital participation by every welder a prerequisite without user approval.

## Comprehensive-review updates

Architecture-critical ownership and attribution are now resolved in A01–A03/D13–D17: the application owns official QC decision history; controlled paper fallback is accepted; the actual signer and acting capacity are recorded. The user also needs to act under formally delegated Picogrid authority. This is a separate capacity backed by recorded scope/effectivity/designation, not automatically granted by system ownership. Owner-only action remains separately attributable and does not turn missing/failed evidence into passed evidence or claim another signer. Concrete delegation before use is B46; organization role/competency eligibility is A06/B19.

Cone shared program visibility is confirmed D19/A05; senior organizational management of own backup grants/revocation and self-approval restrictions is confirmed D20/A06; technical qualifications do not themselves confer approval grants, nor do grants manufacture a technical qualification. Inspection, test validity, coupon acceptance, procedure/operator/machine approval, NCR disposition/closeout, normal release, at-risk approval and shipment execution are separately identified actions. The remaining definitions are tracked under B19/B29/B37, not optional defaults.

Independent serial/joint-operation outcomes and partial shipment within one work order are confirmed D18/A04; a shared job does not broaden another serial's acceptance or clear its restriction. Laboratories initially provide reports without their own accounts (D19); who accepts their technical evidence remains B19.

D22/A08 confirms that incorrect supporting evidence causes dependency flags and pauses further affected approvals/releases/shipments pending authorized scoped review. Cone process authority and Picogrid hardware-disposition authority remain separate; scope and clearance evidence must be defined under B33–B35. Software impact propagation does not itself grant a disposition or manufacture an NCR.
